Abstract: RAIJIN30 is a high-density argon Hall thruster developed from the RAIJIN66 TAL-type thruster by reducing the channel cross-sectional area by a factor of 2.7, increasing the propellant mass flow density by the same factor. The design was guided by a magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) model relating plasma density, acceleration length, and magnetic confinement. Initial argon measurements show higher thrust and improved anode efficiency compared with RAIJIN66 at comparable discharge powers, indicating that MHD-guided geometric scaling can improve the performance of argon Hall thrusters.
