Today we reassembled the thrust stand, which we had temporarily disassembled for cleaning. In the first video you can see how the knife edges of the thrust stand carry the vibration until the end of the video.

{% include diary-video.html file="2026-10-05-thruststandassembly" %}

Afterwards we opened the chamber and started the setup for the Hall thruster experiments. Sueyoshi, a Japanese master's student, and I installed the frame, then the ion collector plate, which we cleaned quickly, and then the thrust stand inside the chamber, including the placement of the cathode. After that, we took some distance measurements to answer a question from a review. Finally, we installed the thrust calibration motor. That is roughly everything we could do without setting up the thruster itself, which will follow next!
