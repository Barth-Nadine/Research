Last week, I installed the thrust mount in the chamber and finished the basic setup, including connecting the lines. We also had undergraduate students visiting for their experiments, so I didn’t get a chance to document the process this time. We showed them a quick overview of the setup and how we pump down the chamber, but there wasn’t enough time to check everything while the chamber was open.

Today, I went back to finish those checks. First, Sueyoshi and I corrected a slightly misaligned cathode orifice. Then we checked all the power supply connections and made sure there were no short circuits. Afterwards, we replaced the faulty mass flow controller, and I adjusted the thrust stand. That part takes a little patience: all the knife edges need to be precisely aligned!

Our next experiment day with the undergraduate students is planned for Thursday. Hopefully, everything will be ready for a successful run!
