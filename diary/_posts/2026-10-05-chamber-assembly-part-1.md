Today, we reassembled the thrust stand after temporarily disassembling it for cleaning. An interesting observation in the first video is that, during the assembly, the knife edges begin to transmit the vibrations.

{% include diary-video.html file="2026-10-05-thruststandassembly" %}

Afterwards, we opened the chamber and began setting up for the Hall thruster experiments. Sueyoshi, a Japanese master’s student, and I installed the frame, followed by the ion collector plate after a quick cleaning. We then installed the thrust stand and positioned the cathode inside the chamber. After that, we measured some distances to address a reviewer’s question. Finally, we installed the thrust calibration motor. This was about everything we could do before installing the thruster itself, which is the next step!
