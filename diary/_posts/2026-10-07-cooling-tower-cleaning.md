Today, we cleaned the cooling tower, which we do roughly once a month. We start by removing the filters and protective screens around the tower. We then drain the water while scrubbing the inside with brushes. Once the dirt has been washed out, we remove as much remaining residue from the bottom as possible before refilling the tower with fresh water.
One person cleans the filters and protective screens separately while the others clean the tower. After refilling, we reinstall the filters and screens and check that everything works properly.
