After the two-week experimental period at the Tsue-Nakasuka Lab ended, I was finally able to reassemble the cathode today to better protect it from oxidation. The video below shows a timelapse of the reassembly, carried out under a continuous 20 SCCM argon flow, followed by a leak check of the Swagelok fittings using Snoop. Finally, I closed the chamber and kept it under 1 Torr to protect it from further oxidation without needing to keep the argon flowing.
